About Us

Racing, done properly.

At Next Orbit Speedfest, we believe that the excitement of motorsport should be accessible to something everyone can experience. Our goal is to bring the adrenaline, competition and atmosphere of professional racing to drivers and spectators across Los Santos, all within a legal, organised and controlled environment.

What We Do

Motorsport for every kind of driver

We host a wide variety of racing events that are designed to appeal to every kind of motorsport enthusiast. From one-off exhibition races and unforgettable automotive experiences, to rally events and large-scale championships, every event offers something different.

Whether drivers are competing for championship glory, testing themselves in a specialist race or simply experiencing a new form of motorsport, we aim to make every moment feel just as exciting and memorable as the last.

More Than Racing

A platform for drivers, teams and businesses

Our events are about more than simply crossing the finish line first. Next Orbit Speedfest provides a place where talented drivers can prove themselves, teams can build their reputations and businesses can connect with the wider automotive community.

We strive to create events that are professionally presented and enjoyable for everyone involved, whether you're taking part or just watching.

Built For The Community

There is always an opportunity waiting on the track.

We welcome experienced racers, rising talent and passionate newcomers alike. No matter what your background or experience is, there's always an opportunity waiting for you on the track. With new event concepts, racing disciplines and experiences constantly being explored by us, Los Santos has never been a better place to experience the thrill of racing in a legal setting.
